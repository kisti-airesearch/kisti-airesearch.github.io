    KISTI–NII/LLMC Joint Workshop

    We were invited to a two-day joint workshop with Japan's National Institute of Informatics (NII) and its Research and Development Center for Large Language Models (LLMC), held in Tokyo on August 27–28. Researchers from both sides shared progress on large language model research.

  • 2026.08.09

    KDD 2026 Showcase — Pushing the Boundaries of AI for Science

    We presented our latest technological advancements at KDD 2026, the premier global conference on data mining and artificial intelligence, held August 9–13 at ICC Jeju.

    At this year's corporate exhibition, the KISTI Large-scale AI Research Center highlighted an end-to-end ecosystem designed to accelerate scientific discovery, automate research workflows, and streamline large language model operations.

    💡 Key Exhibition Highlights

    • 🔬 AI Co-Scientist — autonomous research & discovery. Automatically analyzes vast volumes of scientific literature to synthesize knowledge, generates novel research hypotheses, and suggests promising directions for scientific breakthroughs.
    • 🤖 KONI-WebAgent — document-grounded form filling. Streamlines administrative burdens by parsing multi-modal documents such as resumes and research proposals, then automatically filling out complex web forms across research portals and grant management systems.
    • 🛠️ KONI-Forge — comprehensive LLMOps platform. Built for high-performance computing environments to manage the entire LLM lifecycle, facilitating seamless fine-tuning, model evaluation, dynamic serving, and hyperparameter optimization for specialized scientific models.

    🌐 Building an Open Science Ecosystem

    Beyond standalone agents, we shared our progress in building an open-science platform for researchers and industry partners worldwide — including multimodal LLM (MLLM) architectures for complex document understanding, advanced parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) techniques, and large-scale scientific dataset preprocessing pipelines.
